Remember Who You Are — The Lord Is My Shepherd

Read: Scripture Joshua 14:6–14 NIV
Read alongside: Scripture Psalm 23 NIV

There is something powerful about looking back and seeing how faithfully God has led you.

There are seasons when the path has been clear and seasons when it has not. There are prayers that were answered quickly and others that took years. There are moments of joy, disappointment, waiting, change and unexpected blessing.

And through all of it, God has been faithful.

Psalm 23 begins with one of the simplest and strongest pictures in Scripture:

Scripture Psalm 23:1 NIV

A shepherd does not only protect the sheep. He leads them. He brings them to good places. He restores them. He stays with them on the journey.

That means we are not only people who can look back and say, “God was faithful then.”

We can also say, “God is still leading me now.”

Remember Caleb
#

Caleb had waited a long time.

He had been one of the twelve spies sent into the land many years earlier. While most of the others saw only the size of the obstacles, Caleb trusted that God would be faithful to what he had promised.

Then came years of waiting.

By the time we meet Caleb again in Joshua 14, he is around eighty-five years old. He has lived through the wilderness years, seen a generation pass away and watched God fulfil his promises to Israel.

It would have been understandable for Caleb simply to look back and be thankful.

But that is not what he does.

He says:

Scripture Joshua 14:12 NIV

There is no sense that Caleb thinks his useful years are behind him. He is grateful for what God has done, but he is still looking forward.

He still believes there is something ahead.

Still Looking Forward
#

Caleb’s confidence is not really about strength or personality. It is rooted in the faithfulness of God.

He can look back across decades and say, in effect, the God who kept me then will keep me now.

That is a powerful way to live.

You do not have to pretend that nothing has changed. Life does change. Energy changes. Responsibilities change. The shape of the journey changes.

But change does not mean the journey is over.

There can still be:

  • people to encourage;
  • prayers to pray;
  • wisdom to share;
  • relationships to deepen;
  • faith to pass on;
  • new things to receive from God.

Caleb teaches us that later life is not only about remembering what God has done. It can also be about asking, “Lord, what are you still leading me into?”

The Shepherd Still Leads
#

Psalm 23 is full of movement.

The Shepherd:

  • leads beside quiet waters;
  • refreshes the soul;
  • guides along right paths;
  • walks with us through dark valleys;
  • prepares a table;
  • brings us into his goodness and love.

This is not a picture of a God who brought us safely this far and then stepped away.

It is a picture of a God who keeps leading.

You may not know what every part of the road ahead looks like. Caleb did not know every detail either. But he knew enough to keep trusting the God who had been faithful all his life.

That is enough for today too.

There Is More Ahead
#

Sometimes we can become very good at talking about what God did in the past.

Those stories matter. They remind us of his faithfulness.

But they are not the whole story.

If you are still here, there is still a today to live with God.

There is still kindness to give, encouragement to speak, prayer to offer and love to share.

There are still people who need what God has placed in you.

There may even be new things you have not yet imagined.

God is not finished with you.

Caleb had history with God, but he also had expectation.

That is a beautiful combination: gratitude for what God has done and faith for what he will still do.

Remember
#

Caleb could look back over many years and see the faithfulness of God. That history did not make him passive. It made him confident.

Remember:

  • The Lord is your Shepherd.
  • He has led you faithfully.
  • He is leading you still.
  • There is still purpose ahead.
  • There is still something to give.
  • God is not finished with you.

Say this together
#

The Lord is my Shepherd. He has led me faithfully. He is leading me still. There is more ahead. God is not finished with me.

Prayer
#

Father, thank you for every way you have led me through the years. Thank you for the times I could see what you were doing and for the times when I simply had to trust you.

Thank you for Caleb and for the reminder that a long history with you can become a reason to keep looking forward.

Help me to recognise what you are still doing in my life. Show me where I can encourage, pray, bless and serve. Give me faith for what is ahead, and help me to keep following you one step at a time.

Thank you that you are my Shepherd, that you are with me now, and that you are still leading me.

In Jesus’ name, Amen.
