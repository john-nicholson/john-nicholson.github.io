Remember Who You Are — Greater Is He Who Is in You

Part : Remember Who You Are — Do Not Be Afraid

Read: Scripture 1 John 4:4–18 NIV
Read alongside: Scripture Psalm 56 NIV

Fear can be very convincing. It can make a thought feel certain simply because it feels strong. It can make something small feel enormous, or make us feel that because we are frightened, the thing we fear must have power over us.

John writes to believers with a very different perspective:

Scripture 1 John 4:4 NIV

That is not a promise that frightening things will never happen. It is a reminder about who is greater. Fear is not greater. Confusion is not greater. The things we cannot explain are not greater. Jesus is greater.

John knew what it was to keep walking with Jesus
#

By the time John wrote these words, he had followed Jesus for many years. He had seen extraordinary things, but he had also lived through loss, opposition and uncertainty. He had watched friends die, seen the church go through difficulty, and continued to serve Christ into later life.

And he was still writing. Still encouraging. Still reminding believers of what was true.

John’s age did not mean that God was finished with him. His circumstances did not mean that his purpose was over. In later life, one of the things God used him to do was to help others remember the truth when fear and confusion were pressing in.

That matters for us too. God is not finished with you. You can still pray. You can still encourage. You can still speak of God’s faithfulness. You can still love people well. You can still remind the next generation of what God has done.

When I am afraid
#

Psalm 56 does not pretend that fear is not real. David says plainly:

Scripture Psalm 56:3 NIV

Notice that he does not say, “I am never afraid.” He says, when I am afraid.

Faith is not pretending that fear has disappeared. Sometimes faith is simply turning again towards God while the fear is still there. You may not be able to reason your way through every troubling thought, but you can return to what is true: God is here. Jesus is greater. I belong to him.

Later in 1 John 4, John writes that God’s love drives out fear. The more securely we know that we are loved by God, the less authority fear is allowed to have over us. We are not trying to make ourselves safe enough for God to love us. We are already his.

Remember
#

A frightening thought is still only a thought. A strong feeling is still only a feeling. Neither one changes who God is, and neither one changes who you are in Christ.

The One who lives in you is greater.

And the God who has walked with you through all the years behind you is still with you today. Your story is not finished, your place in his family has not changed, and your life still has purpose.

Say this together
#

I belong to Jesus. God is with me. God has not forgotten me. Greater is he who is in me. God is not finished with me. When I am afraid, I will trust in him.

Prayer
#

Father, thank you that you are greater than anything that frightens me. When fear feels strong, help me remember that fear is not in charge. Jesus is greater, and I belong to him.

Thank you for all the years you have walked with me, and thank you that you are still with me today. Help me to trust you, to rest in your love, and to remember that you are still able to use my life to encourage and bless others.

In Jesus’ name, Amen.
